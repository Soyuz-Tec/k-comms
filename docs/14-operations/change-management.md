# Change Management

Classify changes by risk, require peer review, use progressive delivery, define freeze periods, record emergency changes, and verify rollback. Database, security-policy, and identity changes receive enhanced review and rehearsal.

The normative execution procedure is the
[development-to-production completion standard](development-to-production-completion-standard.md).
After a change is implemented, the owner continues through every applicable
gate and records the required evidence without waiting for a separate release
instruction. Required review, protected-environment approval, and fail-closed
controls remain mandatory.
